# Product vision
**MeHEnG** makes an entrepreneurial idea measurable and actionable.
Audience: solo founders, small teams, builders, early-stage entrepreneurs.

## Four connected pillars
- Discover: idea, hypothesis, experiments, evidence, decision.
- Workspace: canvas, tasks, team, operations, budgeting.
- Network: public profiles, opt-in collaboration, mentor search.
- Growth: launch, customers, metrics, validated growth experiments.

## Differentiation
Evidence and decisions stay linked; task recommendations derive from gaps.
Every pillar references one Venture ID; data ownership remains explicit.

## Product safeguards
- An untested hypothesis is not proof.
- A dashboard score never implies commercial success.
- No public sharing without affirmative consent.
- The app must retain value without paid AI or an internet connection.

## Explicit initial non-goals
AI agent marketplace, payments, enterprise ERP, social feed rankings,
infinite autonomous actions, complex sync, microservices.
