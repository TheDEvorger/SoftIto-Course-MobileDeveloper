# Art direction / HægæRûetn

## Mood

Vivid, elegant, hopeful high-fantasy. A welcoming kingdom that responds to commitment. No oppressive black or gray backgrounds, no clichéd RPG stock icons, no copied anime/game art, no generic parchment pasted across the entire UI. Refined serif display type MAY be evaluated later only if font licensing permits; current seed uses OS fonts and vector painting.

## Primary scene

The currently included `OriginalKingdomArt` paints an original warm skyline, mountain/hill layers, sun, miniature keep, banner, forest, travel path and stars, parametrized by age. This is ORIGINAL source code, not an external image. Later replace/dress it with six separate originally generated and reviewed environmental illustrations.

## 24 insignia

24 badges in six families, four apiece. Visual sophistication grows from raw leaf/compass tokens to royal architectural ornament; avoid overwrought clutter. Every badge should remain legible at 40×40. Use original iconography and an accessibility text label. Material icons in v0.1.0 are temporary glyph stand-ins, NOT the final custom badges.

## Celebration intensity

- Daily habit: micro-glow / seal + XP; under 0.8 sec when polished.
- Badge: central award card, particles.
- Rank change: full-screen triumph, always skippable, limit motion per OS Reduce Motion.
- 24th rank: theatrical proclamation **"Kral öldü! Yaşasın yeni kral!"** in affectionate humor.

## Screens

Bottom bar always has exactly 5 entries: Vazifeler / Ahitler / Meydan / Rehber / Kronik. Center is the social encouragement plaza. Upper herald ribbon carries tips and actions. On small screens, avoid unreadably small tap targets.
